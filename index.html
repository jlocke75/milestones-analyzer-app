Why It Works

Diagnosis Before Instruction

Wrong answers aren't equal. Classroom Growth Coach distinguishes seven failure types — Knowledge, Application, Task Interpretation, Activation, Regulation, Avoidance, and Access Route Mismatch — so the intervention matches the actual problem, not just the score.

Calibrated Feedback

Six Feedback Clusters and four modifier flags ensure every correction lands. What works for a Connective thinker in Cluster D is not what works for a List thinker with an FA flag. Every student gets the kind of feedback they can actually use.

Production Over Recognition

Students often produce correct structures before they can identify them. The Method tests both, separately — surfacing strengths that recognition-only assessments hide and gaps that production-only practice misses.
